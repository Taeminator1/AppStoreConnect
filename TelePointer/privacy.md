# TelePointer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 30, 2026_

TelePointer does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Data Collection

- TelePointer collects no personal information, usage data, analytics, or diagnostics.
- TelePointer makes no network connections.
- Your settings — shortcuts, per-app exceptions, and pointer speed — are stored only on your Mac.
- TelePointer includes no third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising, and shares nothing with third parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Settings are kept only in the app's sandbox container on your Mac, `~/Library/Containers/com.taeminyun.TelePointer`. Deleting this folder removes them.

## Accessibility Permission

TelePointer asks for Accessibility permission only to post mouse click events at the pointer's location. It does not read, record, or inspect the content or interface of other apps.

You can revoke this permission at any time in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility**.

## Files

TelePointer reads or writes a settings file only when you choose one with **Import Settings…** or **Export Settings…**. It does not access any other files.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
